Brazilians showing the world as it really is

After more than 15 years of traveling, we created this space to share what we live along the way: places worth the trip, local food that tells a story and the details tourists almost never notice.

Today we live in northern Germany, and we also show the inside of this life — the weekly grocery run, the bureaucracy, the habits that surprise any Brazilian and the small towns nobody talks about.

No advertising script, no “everything is perfect”: the idea is for you to feel each place as if you were right there with us.
